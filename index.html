Everything You Need to Know About PEG

Playful Earth Gamers (PEG) is a global Play & Earn gaming ecosystem and digital technology platform connecting gamers across Mobile, PC, Consoles, and Game Centers through competitive tournaments, live predictions, digital reward cards, and secure financial services.
Members sign up via Firebase authentication, verify their account status with a $1 fee via Paystack, and unlock access to global leaderboards, instant cash withdrawals, virtual payment cards, and hardware loan financing.
The $1 verification fee prevents automated bot spam, ensures a fair competitive tournament ecosystem, verifies your location for region-based payouts, and complies with global KYC/AML security standards.
Through our Paystack payment gateway, we support local and international Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), Mobile Money (MoMo across Africa), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and direct bank transfers.
Coupons are awarded for winning arena challenges, daily logins, and ranking on global leaderboards. They can be redeemed inside your Universal Wallet for cash payouts, entry passes, or gaming equipment discounts.
It is a multi-currency live digital vault built into your profile. It holds tournament cash prize payouts, reward tokens, deposits, and allows instant cashouts to your local bank or virtual card.
Every verified member can generate a PEG Virtual Debit Mastercard/Visa linked directly to their wallet balance, usable worldwide for online purchases, gaming subscriptions, or ATM withdrawals.

Cashouts are processed automatically in real-time via BaaS webhooks to your linked bank account, Mobile Money wallet, or directly loaded onto your PEG Virtual Debit Card.
All user data and financial assets are secured by 256-bit SSL encryption, Firebase Auth security protocols, PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS), and PCI DSS Level 1 payment compliance.
