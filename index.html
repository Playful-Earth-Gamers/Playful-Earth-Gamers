Welcome to Playful Earth Gamers (PEG)

Playful Earth Gamers (PEG) is a next-generation global Play & Earn gaming ecosystem where players and gaming fans connect, compete, support, and earn in one trusted community. Whether you're a casual gamer, professional competitor, esports fan, or tournament supporter, PEG creates opportunities for everyone to participate, grow, and enjoy competitive gaming.

Our mission is to build the world's most trusted gaming platform by delivering fair tournaments, live challenges, global leaderboards, achievements, secure rewards, and an engaging community that recognizes skill, dedication, and sportsmanship.

Players can join tournaments, complete daily challenges, earn PEG Coins, unlock achievements, climb worldwide rankings, receive cash rewards, and build a verified gaming reputation across mobile, PC, and console games.

Gaming fans can follow live competitions, support their favorite players and teams, watch exciting matches, and where permitted by law, participate in regulated prediction or wagering features. Supporters may back one or multiple players or teams on a single prediction slip, while players can choose whether or not to participate in eligible reward-based prediction programs.

At PEG, every match creates opportunity, every achievement tells a story, and every victory brings players one step closer to becoming part of a global community built on competition, fairness, innovation, and real rewards.

import { createClient } from '@supabase/supabase-js';

export const supabase = createClient(
  process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL!,
  process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY!
);

// Subscribe to live SaaS Leaderboard update stream
export function subscribeLeaderboard(onUpdate) {
  return supabase
    .channel('global-rankings')
    .on('postgres_changes', { event: 'UPDATE', schema: 'public', table: 'leaderboard' }, 
      (payload) => onUpdate(payload.new)
    )
    .subscribe();
}
